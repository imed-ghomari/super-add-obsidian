## SuperAdd 1.1.1 — review fixes

A small follow-up to 1.1.0 addressing Obsidian community review feedback. No new features to learn.

### What you'll notice
- The first settings section is now called **Task defaults** (previously "General"), so it's clearer what it holds: your default folder and the natural-language toggle. It also shows up better in Settings search.
- Same searchable settings as 1.1.0: global Settings search finds every option, with quick filters inside Properties and Templates.

### Please note
- This version requires **Obsidian 1.13.1 or newer** (the searchable settings need it). If Obsidian asks you to update first, that's why — your tasks and settings carry over unchanged.

### Action needed
- None, unless your Obsidian is older than 1.13.1 — then just update Obsidian first.
