## SuperAdd 1.2.1 — highlighting alignment fix

Fixes the highlighting sitting to the right of the task text. The colored backgrounds now sit directly behind the recognized words.

### What's fixed
- The highlight layer now uses the same start alignment as the textarea instead of inheriting the settings row's end alignment.
- Long lines and wrapped lines stay aligned with the text they describe.
- Highlight colors, editing, selection, caret, and scrolling are otherwise unchanged.

### Compatibility
- No settings or template changes are needed. Existing tasks, custom properties, and templates keep working.
- Requires Obsidian 1.13.1 or newer.
